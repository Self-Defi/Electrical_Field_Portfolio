# Electrical Field Portfolio

A visual portfolio documenting hands-on electrical field experience across commercial and residential environments.

This portfolio was developed to provide employers, contractors, and hiring managers with a quick view of practical electrical skills supported by actual field photos and video.

## Field Experience

Current documented experience includes:

- EMT conduit installation and bending
- Commercial wire pulling and conductor installation
- Electrical rough-in
- Boxes and raceway systems
- Receptacle and switch installation
- Device termination
- Circuit identification
- Residential NM-B / Romex installation
- Commercial THHN conductor installation
- Low-voltage CAT5e cabling
- Electrical troubleshooting and repair
- Jobsite material and system identification

## Portfolio Structure

The portfolio is intentionally organized by **field skill rather than employer or classroom course**.

Each primary skill section contains selected visual evidence of hands-on work, including:

**01 — EMT / Conduit**  
Commercial raceway installation, routing, bending, and support.

**02 — Wire Pulling / Conductors**  
Feeding and pulling conductors through commercial raceway systems.

**03 — Boxes / Rough-In**  
Electrical boxes, raceways, branch-circuit pathways, and active construction rough-in.

**04 — Devices / Terminations**  
Receptacles, switches, conductor preparation, connections, and device installation.

**05 — Data / CAT5e**  
Low-voltage cable routing and installation during commercial construction.

## Technical Development

Field experience is supplemented by electrical and construction coursework and continued technical training.

The objective is to continuously connect classroom knowledge with practical installation, troubleshooting, code awareness, and jobsite workmanship.

## Purpose

This repository supports a QR-accessible portfolio designed to accompany an electrical apprentice resume.

Rather than relying exclusively on written skill descriptions, the portfolio provides direct visual evidence of work performed and experience gained in the field.
